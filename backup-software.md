# Backup & sync software

- [Syncthing](https://syncthing.net/) — Syncs folders between your own computers in real time over LAN or internet; no central cloud, you pair devices and data stays on machines you control.

- [Backblaze](https://www.backblaze.com/) — **Computer Backup** for continuous cloud backup of a Mac or PC; **B2** is S3-compatible object storage if you need cheap cloud buckets for apps or `restic`/tools.

- [restic](https://restic.net/) — Command-line backups with encryption and deduplication to local disks, SFTP, or cloud (S3, B2, etc.); good for scripted, verifiable restores and pairing with your own storage.

- [Sync.com pricing (individual)](https://www.sync.com/pricing-individual/) — End-to-end encrypted cloud storage with sync and sharing; privacy-oriented alternative to mainstream drive products when you want encrypted cloud + collaboration.

- [Proton Drive pricing](https://proton.me/drive/pricing) — End-to-end encrypted cloud storage from Proton; fits if you want Drive bundled with Mail, VPN, and other Proton services under one subscription.
